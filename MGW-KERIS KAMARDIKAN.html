KERIS KAMARDIKAN

KERIS KAMARDIKAN Keris merupakan pusaka yang tergambar / tervisualisasi ke dalam bentuk dan nama dapur, motif pamor, bahan bakunya dan ukuran anatomis. Keris adalah kaca benggala/cermin dari rasa, pola piker, sikap, tatanan hidup, filsafah, hingga pemahaman tentang ketuhanan (Konsep Lingga Yoni). Keris dibagi menjadi dua bentuk - Keris Lurus/Lajer/Leres. Keris ini adalah simbol diam /posisi meditasi/kuda – kuda siap. - Keris Lekuk/Luk. Keris ini merupakan simbol liku – liku kehidupan dan tingkatan ujian yang harus dilalui manusia. Bentuk lekuk keris selalu ganjil menandakan manusia harus senantiasa berusaha tanpa henti, berjuang hingga memperoleh bentuk yang genap. Simbol perjuangan tanpa kata menyerah. Adapun numerologi keris berdasarkan lekuknya adalah: 1. Keris Luk 3 dinamakan Keris Jangkung 2. Keris Luk 5 dinamakan Keris Pandawa 3. Keris Luk 7 dinamakan keris Carubuk 4. Keris Luk 9 dinamakan Keris Sempono atau Sempaner 5. Keris Luk 11 dinamakan Keris Carito 6. Keris Luk 13 dinamakan Keris Sengkelat Keris di Museum Gubug Wayang merupakan keris Kamardikan yang ditempa oleh Bp Andik Fathur Rosyid dari Sumenep, Madura. Seperti halnya pusaka kamardikan lainnya, keris kamardikan merupakan simbol merdeka nya bangsa Indonesia dalam berbudaya tanpa dibayang – bayangi oleh kolonialisme. Fungsi keris pun pada masa kini bergeser tidak hanya sebagai Ageman dan Piyandel saja, akan tetapi sering dipakai sebagai aksesori pengantin pria pada acara pernikahan dan ritus budaya yang masih dilestarikan di masyarakat.

A keris is a traditional Indonesian heirloom weapon whose characteristics are expressed through its shape and dhapur (blade form), pamor (distinctive patterns on the blade), raw materials, and anatomical dimensions. The keris serves as a reflection of human emotions, thought patterns, attitudes, ways of life, philosophy, and understanding of divinity, as embodied in the concept of Lingga-Yoni. The keris is generally classified into two forms: Straight Keris (Keris Lurus/Lajer/Leres). This type symbolizes stillness, a meditative posture, or a stance of readiness. Wavy Keris (Keris Lekuk/Luk). This type symbolizes the complexities of human life and the various levels of trials that individuals must overcome. The number of curves on a keris is always odd, symbolizing the idea that human beings must continuously strive and persevere without giving up until they attain completeness. It represents an enduring spirit of struggle and perseverance. The numerological classification of the keris based on the number of its curves is as follows: A keris with three curves (Luk 3) is known as Keris Jangkung. A keris with five curves (Luk 5) is known as Keris Pandawa. A keris with seven curves (Luk 7) is known as Keris Carubuk. A keris with nine curves (Luk 9) is known as Keris Sempono or Keris Sempaner. A keris with eleven curves (Luk 11) is known as Keris Carito. A keris with thirteen curves (Luk 13) is known as Keris Sengkelat. The keris in the collection of the Gubug Wayang Museum is a Keris Kamardikan forged by Mr. Andik Fathur Rasyid from Sumenep, Madura. Like other Kamardikan heirlooms, the Keris Kamardikan symbolizes Indonesia’s independence in expressing and developing its cultural identity without the constraints of colonial influence. In contemporary society, the function of the keris has evolved beyond its traditional roles as an ageman (an object worn as part of traditional attire) and piyandel (a symbol of spiritual confidence and personal conviction). Today, it is also frequently used as an accessory worn by grooms during wedding ceremonies and other cultural rituals that continue to be preserved within local communities. <\p>
