GURU DURNA/DRONA

GURU DURNO/DRONA Drona dilahirkan dalam keluarga brahmana (kaum pendeta Hindu). Ia merupakan putera dari pendeta Bharadwaja, lahir di kota yang sekarang disebut Dehradun (modifikasi dari kata dehra-dron, guci tanah liat), yang berarti bahwa ia (Drona) berkembang bukan di dalam rahim, namun di luar tubuh manusia, yakni dalam Droon (tong atau guci). Kisah kelahiran Drona diceritakan secara dramatis dalam Mahabharata. Bharadwaja pergi bersama rombongannya menuju Gangga untuk melakukan penyucian diri. Di sana ia melihat bidadari yang sangat cantik datang untuk mandi. Sang pendeta dikuasai nafsu, menyebabkannya mengeluarkan air mani yang sangat banyak. Ia mengatur supaya air mani tersebut ditampung dalam sebuah pot yang disebut drona, dan dari cairan tersebut Drona lahir kemudian dirawat. Drona kemudian bangga bahwa ia lahir dari Bharadwaja tanpa pernah berada di dalam rahim. Drona menghabiskan masa mudanya dalam kemiskinan, namun belajar agama dan militer bersama-sama dengan pangeran dari Kerajaan Panchala bernama Drupada. Drupada dan Drona kemudian menjadi teman dekat dan Drupada, dalam masa kecilnya yang bahagia, berjanji untuk memberikan setengah kerajaannya kepada Drona pada saat menjadi Raja Panchala.

Drona was born into a Brahmin family, a social class traditionally associated with Hindu priests and religious teachers. He was the son of the sage Bharadwaja and was born in a city now known as Dehradun. The name Dehradun is believed to derive from the words Dehra-Dron, referring to an earthen vessel or pot. This reflects the belief that Drona did not develop in a mother’s womb but came into existence outside the human body, namely in a vessel known as a drona. The story of Drona’s birth is dramatically recounted in the Mahabharata. Bharadwaja once travelled with his companions to the Ganges River to perform a ritual purification. There, he saw an exceptionally beautiful celestial nymph (apsara) arriving to bathe. Overcome by desire, the sage emitted a considerable amount of semen. He arranged for it to be collected in a vessel called a drona. Drona was subsequently born from this substance and raised. Drona took pride in the fact that he was born from Bharadwaja without ever having developed in a mother’s womb. He spent his youth in poverty but studied religion and military arts alongside Drupada, a prince of the Kingdom of Panchala. Drona and Drupada later became close friends. During their happy childhood, Drupada promised that, once he became King of Panchala, he would give Drona half of his kingdom. <\p>
