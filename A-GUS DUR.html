GUS DUR

Dr. (H.C.) K.H. Abdurrahman Wahid, born Abdurrahman ad-Dakhil on September 7, 1940, in Denanyar, Jombang, East Java, and widely known as Gus Dur, was an Indonesian Muslim leader and politician who served as the fourth President of Indonesia from 1999 to 2001. He succeeded President B. J. Habibie after being elected by the People's Consultative Assembly (MPR) following the 1999 general election. His administration was assisted by the National Unity Cabinet. Abdurrahman Wahid's presidency began on October 20, 1999, and ended during the Special Session of the People's Consultative Assembly (MPR) in 2001. On July 23, 2001, he was succeeded by Megawati Soekarnoputri after his mandate was revoked by the MPR. Abdurrahman Wahid was the former Chairman of the Tanfidziyah (executive body) of Nahdlatul Ulama and the founder of the National Awakening Party (PKB). He was a pioneer of multiculturalism in the Indonesian archipelago. Gus Dur also reinstated the public celebration of Chinese New Year and Chinese cultural traditions, which had been restricted during the New Order administration. He passed away on December 30, 2009, at the age of 69 and was buried at Tebuireng Islamic Boarding School, Jombang, East Java.

Ia menggantikan Presiden B.J. Habibie setelah dipilih oleh Majelis Permusyawaratan Rakyat (MPR) hasil Pemilu 1999. Penyelenggaraan pemerintahannya dibantu oleh Kabinet Persatuan Nasional. Tepat 23 Juli 2001, kepemimpinannya digantikan oleh Megawati Soekarnoputri setelah mandatnya dicabut oleh MPR.
