WAYANG PRING

WAYANG PRING/BAMBU Berpegang pada falsafah bambu, Ki Sri Widodo, dalang asal Desa Balamoa, Kecamatan Pangkah, Kabupaten Tegal, Jawa Tengah, mencipta wayang pring atau wayang bambu dan menjadikannya sebagai salah satu khazanah budaya di Indonesia. Wayang pring juga menjadi media penyampai pesan moral kepada masyarakat. Lahir di Kabupaten Tegal pada 3 September 1974, Ki Widodo memperoleh bakat seni dari sesepuhnya. Kakeknya, mendiang Ki Suwati, merupakan dalang wayang kulit yang terkenal di Tegal. Begitu pula ayahnya, Ki Gunawan, juga seorang dalang. Ayahnya adalah kakak kandung dalang wayang suket, mendiang Ki Slamet Gundono. Tumbuh di lingkungan seniman, sejak kecil Widodo telah memiliki kecintaan pada dunia seni karawitan dan wayang. Saat sekolah dasar, dia bergabung dengan kelompok karawitan di sekolahnya. Karena itu, selepas sekolah menengah pertama (SMP) pada 1992, dia memilih melanjutkan pendidikan ke Sekolah Menengah Karawitan Indonesia (SMKI) di Solo. Lepas dari SMKI, dia melanjutkan pendidikan ke Sekolah Tinggi Seni Indonesia (STSI) Surakarta dengan mengambil jurusan karawitan. Di sela-sela kegiatannya, Ki Widodo berupaya menggali kemampuannya yang lain. Saat hendak memenuhi tugas untuk ujian kelulusan jenjang S-1 pada 2003, dia membuat kreasi bunyi gamelan dari perpaduan karet dan bambu. Dari situlah, dia menemukan ide membuat wayang dari bambu. Ki Widodo melihat falsafah yang kuat dari tanaman ini. Setiap batang bambu selalu memberikan kesempatan kepada batang lain untuk tumbuh sendiri. Jadi, kalau sudah ada tunas atau rebung yang tumbuh, tidak akan ada tunas lain yang tumbuh di tempat yang sama. Meskipun demikian, bambu tidak pernah hidup sendiri, tetapi bergerombol dalam satu kelompok. Batang bambu yang menjulang tinggi tidak akan pernah bisa roboh ke tanah karena disangga batang-batang bambu lain di kelompok itu. Meskipun tumbuh satu-satu, tanaman bambu bisa kuat karena hidup bersama dan saling menyangga. Pada 11 Maret 2005, untuk pertama kalinya wayang pring mendapat kesempatan pentas di Taman Budaya Surakarta. Aktivitas pementasan pun terus berjalan. Hingga pada 2007, kelompok wayang pring vakum karena pengiringnya kembali ke daerah masing-masing. Pada 2010, Ki Widodo kembali ke Tegal dan menghidupkan kembali wayang pring. Hingga kini, dia terus mementaskan wayang pring dalam berbagai kesempatan. Widodo membentuk Komunitas Wayang Pring Tegal dengan personel antara lain guru dan pegawai tata usaha di SMP Negeri 1 Bojong, Kabupaten Tegal, dan alumni sekolah tersebut. Untuk menarik penonton, Ki Widodo memodifikasi wayang pring sehingga kepala wayang itu bisa digerakkan ke kanan dan ke kiri seperti wayang golek. Upaya mempercantik wayang dengan membentuk wajah dan hiasan pada bagian kepala wayang terus dilakukan.

Guided by the philosophy of bamboo, Ki Sri Widodo, a dalang (puppeteer) from Balamoa Village, Pangkah District, Tegal Regency, Central Java, created Wayang Pring, or bamboo puppetry, establishing it as a distinctive part of Indonesia’s cultural heritage. Wayang Pring also serves as a medium for conveying moral messages to the community. Born in Tegal Regency on September 3, 1974, Ki Widodo inherited his artistic talent from previous generations of his family. His grandfather, the late Ki Suwati, was a renowned wayang kulit puppeteer in Tegal. His father, Ki Gunawan, was also a dalang. His father was the biological elder brother of the late Ki Slamet Gundono, a prominent wayang suket puppeteer. Growing up in an artistic environment, Widodo developed a passion for traditional Javanese music (karawitan) and puppetry from an early age. During elementary school, he joined his school’s karawitan ensemble. After completing junior high school in 1992, he decided to continue his education at the Indonesian School of Traditional Music (Sekolah Menengah Karawitan Indonesia/SMKI) in Solo. After graduating from SMKI, he pursued further studies In karawitan at the Indonesian Institute of the Arts (Sekolah Tinggi Seni Indonesia/STSI) in Surakarta. Alongside his other activities, Ki Widodo sought to explore and develop his additional artistic abilities. In 2003, while preparing an assignment for his undergraduate graduation examination, he created a gamelan sound instrument by combining rubber and bamboo. This experiment inspired him to develop the idea of making puppets from bamboo. Ki Widodo recognised a profound philosophy in the growth of bamboo. Each bamboo stalk allows other stalks to grow alongside it. Once a new shoot, or rebung, emerges, no other shoot grows in precisely the same spot. Nevertheless, bamboo never grows in isolation; instead, it grows in clusters. A tall bamboo stalk does not easily fall to the ground because it is supported by the other stalks in its cluster. Although each stalk grows individually, bamboo becomes strong through living together and supporting one another. On March 11, 2005, Wayang Pring was performed publicly for the first time at Taman Budaya Surakarta. Performances continued in the following years. However, in 2007, the Wayang Pring group temporarily ceased its activities because its accompanying musicians returned to their respective hometowns. In 2010, Ki Widodo returned to Tegal and revived Wayang Pring. Since then, he has continued to stage performances on various occasions. Widodo established the Tegal Wayang Pring Community, whose members include teachers, administrative staff from SMP Negeri 1 Bojong in Tegal Regency, and alumni of the school. To attract audiences, Ki Widodo modified the bamboo puppets so that their heads could move from side to side, similar to wayang golek (three-dimensional wooden puppets). He has also continued to enhance their appearance by shaping their facial features and adding decorative ornaments to their heads. <\p>
