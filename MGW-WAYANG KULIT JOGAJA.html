WAYANG KULIT JOGJAKARTA

WAYANG KULIT GAGRAK JOGJA

Wayang kulit adalah seni tradisional Indonesia yang terutama berkembang di Jawa. Wayang berasal dari kata ‘Ma Hyang’ yang artinya menuju kepada roh spiritual, dewa, atau Tuhan Yang Maha Esa. Adapun pertunjukan ini telah berusia lebih dari setengah milenium. Kemunculannya memiliki cerita tersendiri, terkait denga kepercayaan leluhur, agama hindu – budha hingga masuknya Islam Jawa. Pagelaran wayang kulit dimainkan oleh seorang yang penghibur terhebat di dunia. Bagaimana tidak, selama semalam suntuk dari jam 8 hingga jam 4 pagi, sang dalang memainkan seluruh karakter aktor wayang kulit yang merupakan orang-orangan berbahan kulit kerbau dengan dihias motif hasil kerajinan tatah sungging (ukir kulit). Ia harus mengubah karakter suara, berganti intonasi, mengeluarkan guyonan dan bahkan menyanyi. Untuk menghidupkan suasana, dalang dibantu oleh musisi yang memainkan gamelan atau disebut pengrawit dan para sinden yang menyanyikan lagu-lagu Jawa. Koleksi yang ada di tempat ini merupakan Wayang Kulit Gagrag Jogja. Wayang Kulit Gagrag Jogja atau Wayang Kulit Gaya Yogyakarta merupakan wayang kulit yang secara morfologi memiliki ciri bangun, pola tatahan, dan sunggingan (pewarnaan) yang khas. Selain itu dalam pertunjukan Wayang Kulit Gagrag Jogja juga memiliki unsur-unsur khas yaitu, lakon wayang (penyajian alur kisah dan maknanya), catur ( narasi dan percakapan) , karawitan ( gendhing, sulukan dan properti panggung ).

Wayang kulit is a traditional Indonesian art form that developed primarily on the island of Java. The word wayang is believed to derive from the phrase Ma Hyang, meaning to approach or move towards spiritual beings, deities, or God Almighty. This performing art has a history spanning more than half a millennium. Its emergence is associated with various historical and cultural influences, ranging from ancestral beliefs and Hindu-Buddhist traditions to the arrival and development of Islam in Java. A wayang kulit performance is led by a puppeteer known as a dalang, who is often regarded as one of the world’s most versatile performers. During an all-night performance, typically lasting from 8 p.m. until 4 a.m., the dalang brings every puppet character to life. The puppets are made from buffalo hide and decorated with intricate patterns using the traditional leather-carving and painting techniques known as tatah sungging. The dalang must master a wide range of skills, including changing voices to portray different characters, varying intonation, delivering jokes, and even singing. To create an engaging atmosphere, the dalang is accompanied by musicians who play the gamelan ensemble, known as pengrawit, as well as female singers called sinden, who perform traditional Javanese songs. The collection displayed here consists of Wayang Kulit Gagrag Jogja, also known as Wayang Kulit Gaya Yogyakarta (Yogyakarta-style shadow puppets). Morphologically, this style is distinguished by its characteristic puppet forms, carving patterns (tatahan), and decorative colouring (sunggingan). In addition, performances of Wayang Kulit Gagrag Jogja feature several distinctive elements, including lakon wayang (the presentation of the storyline and its meaning), catur (narration and dialogue), and karawitan (the musical elements of the performance, including gendhing and sulukan, as well as stage properties). <\p>
