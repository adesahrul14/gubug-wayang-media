JENDRAL SUDIRMAN

General of the Army (Posthumous) Raden Soedirman was a senior Indonesian military officer during the Indonesian National Revolution. As the first Commander-in-Chief of the Indonesian National Armed Forces, he was a figure who was respected in Indonesia. Born to an ordinary family in Purbalingga, Dutch East Indies, Soedirman was adopted by his uncle, who was a member of the Javanese aristocracy. Soedirman was well known for his strategy of total guerrilla warfare, and he led the war from 1946 to 1949. He passed away due to tuberculosis, from which he had suffered, on January 29, 1950, and was buried at Semaki Public Cemetery, Yogyakarta. Soedirman's death became a source of grief for all Indonesians. Flags were flown at half-mast, and thousands of people gathered to witness the funeral ceremony. Soedirman continues to be honored by the Indonesian people. His guerrilla resistance was established as a means of developing esprit de corps for the Indonesian military, and the 100-kilometer (62 mi) guerrilla route that he traveled must be followed by Indonesian military cadets before graduating from the Military Academy. Soedirman was featured on the 1968 rupiah banknote, and his name has been commemorated in the names of numerous streets, universities, museums, and monuments. On December 10, 1964, he was officially designated as a National Hero of Indonesia.

JENDERAL SUDIRMAN

Jenderal Besar TNI (Anumerta) Raden Soedirman adalah seorang perwira tinggi Indonesia pada masa Revolusi Nasional Indonesia. Sebagai Panglima Besar Tentara Nasional Indonesia pertama, ia adalah sosok yang dihormati di Indonesia. Terlahir dari pasangan rakyat biasa di Purbalingga, Hindia Belanda, Soedirman diadopsi oleh pamannya yang seorang priyayi. Sudirman terkenal dengan siasat perang semesta griliya, sudirman memimpin perang mulai tahun tahun 1946 – 1949. Dan ia wafat karena penyakit TBC yang dirasakanya, wafat pada tanggal 29 januari 1950 dan di makamkan di TPU semaki yogyakarta. Kematian Soedirman menjadi duka bagi seluruh rakyat Indonesia. Bendera setengah tiang dikibarkan dan ribuan orang berkumpul untuk menyaksikan prosesi upacara pemakaman. Soedirman terus dihormati oleh rakyat Indonesia. Perlawanan gerilyanya ditetapkan sebagai sarana pengembangan esprit de corps bagi tentara Indonesia, dan rute gerilya sepanjang 100-kilometer (62 mi) yang ditempuhnya harus diikuti oleh taruna Indonesia sebelum lulus dari Akademi Militer. Soedirman ditampilkan dalam uang kertas rupiah keluaran 1968, dan namanya diabadikan menjadi nama sejumlah jalan, universitas, museum, dan monumen. Pada tanggal 10 Desember 1964, ia ditetapkan sebagai Pahlawan Nasional Indonesia.
