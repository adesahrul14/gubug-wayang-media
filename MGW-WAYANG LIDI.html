WAYANG LIDI

WAYANG LIDI/SODO Wayang Sodo, salah satu aset seni budaya yang ada di Gunungkidul ini memiliki keunikan tersendiri, seperti namanya, wayang ini terbuat dari Sodo (lidi). Hasil karya warga Padukuhan Gunungbang, Desa Budaya Bejiharjo, Kecamatan Karangmojo ini menjadi ragam baru di dunia seni pewayangan. Dilatarbelakangi atas kecintaannya terhadap seni pertunjukan wayang, sehingga Marsono, lelaki kelahiran 1948 ini berkeingingan menjadi seorang Dalang. Berbagai keterbatasan termasuk juga ekonomi membuatnya tidak mampu mencapai cita-citanya itu. Memendam keinginan cukup lama, akhirnya ia mengekspresikan minatnya dengan anak-anak panti asuhan yang ia kelola. Di waktu senggang ia membuat wayang dari batang rumput dan mengajak anak-anak bermain wayang. Lama kelamaan tak hanya dari batang rumput saja, ia juga buat dari tangkai ketela dan media lain yang mudah ditemukan di sekitar. Dari situ ia mulai berfikir, karena bahan pembuatan wayang yang digunakan hanya sekali pakai ia berencana membuat dengan bahan yang lebih awet. Tahun 2011 lalu Mbah Marsono membuat wayang pertama kalinya menggunakan lidi, keinginan menjadi dalang Wayang Kulit tak kesampaian, beliaupun membuat wayang dengan kreatifitas dan bahan yang didapat dari sekitar rumahnya. Ia memaparkan, Wayang Sodo terbuat dari lidi muda yang dianyam sedemikian rupa sehingga membentuk karakter atau figur-figur dalam pewayangan. Tokoh atau karakter wayang masih mengacu pada figur pewayangan kisah Mahabarata dan Ramayana serta legenda-legenda di masyarakat Jawa. Wayang Sodo merupakan wayang kreasi bertema ramah lingkungan, go green, selain menggunakan bahan-bahan alam, cerita wayang juga mengangkat tema lingkungan dalam pementasannya Selain mahir membuat Wayang Sodo, Mbah Marsono juga lihai memainkannya, beberapa figur wayang juga ia ciptakan melengkapi keunikan kreasinya itu. Nama-nama tokoh diambil dari nama bagian pohon kelapa dengan sebutan Bahasa Jawa yang telah dimodifikasi untuk mewakili watak tokoh atau dengan tujuan aspek kepatutan dan keselarasan nama di dunia wayang.

Wayang Sodo, one of the cultural and artistic heritage assets of Gunungkidul, possesses a distinctive character. As its name suggests, these puppets are made from sodo (palm leaf ribs). Created by residents of Padukuhan Gunungbang, Bejiharjo Cultural Village, Karangmojo District, this art form represents a new variation in the world of traditional Indonesian puppetry. The creation of Wayang Sodo was inspired by Marsono, a man born in 1948, and his deep passion for traditional wayang performances. His love for this art form led him to aspire to become a dalang (puppeteer). However, various limitations, including financial difficulties, prevented him from achieving this ambition. After keeping this aspiration for many years, he eventually found a way to express his interest through the children at the orphanage he managed. In his spare time, he made puppets from grass stems and invited the children to play with them. Over time, he began experimenting with other readily available materials, including cassava stalks and various objects found in the surrounding area. This experience led him to consider using more durable materials, as the materials initially used to make the puppets were disposable. In 2011, Mbah Marsono created his first puppets using palm leaf ribs (lidi). Although his dream of becoming a wayang kulit puppeteer had not come true, he found another way to pursue his passion by creating puppets from materials readily available around his home. According to Marsono, Wayang Sodo is made from young palm leaf ribs that are woven and arranged into various puppet characters and figures. The characters are based on those found in the Mahabharata and Ramayana, as well as legends from Javanese society. Wayang Sodo is an environmentally friendly creative form of puppetry with a go green theme. In addition to using natural materials, its performances also incorporate environmental themes into their stories. Besides being skilled at making Wayang Sodo, Mbah Marsono is also adept at performing with the puppets. He has created several additional puppet figures to further enrich the uniqueness of his work. The names of these characters are derived from the names of different parts of the coconut tree, using modified Javanese terms to represent each character’s personality and to ensure that their names are appropriate and harmonious within the world of wayang. <\p>
