SERIAL SI UNYIL

FILM SERIAL SI UNYIL Si Unyil merupakan salah satu animasi ciptaan Drs. Suyadi atau yang akrab dipanggil dengan sebutan Pak Raden. Serial Si Unyil tayang di TVRI untuk pertama kalinya pada tanggal 5 April 1981. kisah Boneka Produksi Film Negara (PFN) ini sebenarnya sudah digagas sejak 1979. Serial ini menjadi tontonan favorit anak-anak pada era 1980 -1990an. Selain Unyil terdapat beberapa tokoh lainnya, yaitu Usro, Ucrit, Meilani, Pak Raden, Pak Ogah, Ableh, bahkan Mbok Bariah. Berkat animasi ciptaannya, Pak Raden diberikan sejumlah penghargaan. Drs Suyadi atau yang dikenal sebagai tokoh Pak Raden di serial “si unyil” dianugerahi penghargaan Ganesha Widya Jasa Utama karena telah menunjukkan jasa dan prestasi yang menonjol sebagai pelopor Bidang industry Kreatif Klaster Animasi dan Tokoh Animator di tingkat nasional, pada Pada peringatan 92 tahun Pendidikan Tinggi Teknik di Indonesia. Dunia si Unyil adalah Indonesia. Disana ada beragam karakter yang mereprentasikan wajah-wajah Keindonesiaan. Kisah si Unyil dengan setia menemani hari Minggu anak-anak selama 13 tahun hingga 1993. Kemudian cerita Si Unyil kembali tayang pada 2002-2003 oleh stasiun TV swasta dengan nama baru, Laptop Si Unyil. Kesederhanaan cerita, tokoh-tokoh yang dekat dengan keseharian masyarakat dan dukungan pemerintah saat itu membuat cerita Si Unyil tetap selalu dikenang. Hingga kini, belum ada lagi program kartun anak khas Indonesia yang mampu menandingi kepopuleran cerita Si Unyil. Selain Drs. Suyadi, ada 3 orang yang memiliki andil luar biasa juga dalam pembuatan boneka Serial Si Unyil. Pak Nana Ruslana, Pak Nano dan Pak Maman. Koleksi Si Unyil Museum Gubug Wayang merupakan koleksi yang diserah terimakan secara langsung oleh Drs. Suyadi dan keluarga besar beliau secara bertahap, mulai tahun 2015 hingga sekarang. Adapun bentuk awal dari tokoh – tokoh yang ada, digoreskan dalam bentuk sketsa ekspresi dengan bingkai pigura di Museum Gubug Wayang.

Si Unyil is one of the animated puppet series created by Drs. Suyadi, affectionately known as Pak Raden. The series was first broadcast on TVRI on April 5, 1981. This puppet-based production by the State Film Corporation (Perusahaan Film Negara/PFN) had actually been conceived as early as 1979. It became one of the favourite television programmes among Indonesian children throughout the 1980s and 1990s. In addition to Unyil, the series featured several other characters, including Usro, Ucrit, Meilani, Pak Raden, Pak Ogah, Ableh, and Mbok Bariah. Through his creative contributions to the series, Pak Raden received several awards. Drs. Suyadi, widely recognised for portraying the character Pak Raden in Si Unyil, was awarded the Ganesha Widya Jasa Utama Award in recognition of his outstanding contributions and achievements as a pioneer in the creative industry, particularly in the field of animation, and as a prominent national animator. The award was presented during the commemoration of the 92nd anniversary of higher technical education in Indonesia. The world of Si Unyil reflects the diversity of Indonesia. Its characters represent various aspects of Indonesian society and everyday life. For 13 years, until 1993, the stories of Si Unyil faithfully accompanied children on their Sunday mornings. The series returned to television in 2002–2003 through a private television station under a new title, Laptop Si Unyil. The simplicity of its stories, its relatable characters, and the support it received from the government at the time have ensured that Si Unyil remains a memorable part of Indonesian popular culture. To this day, few Indonesian children's animated programmes have achieved the same level of popularity as Si Unyil. Besides Drs. Suyadi, three other individuals made significant contributions to the creation of the Si Unyil puppets: Mr. Nana Ruslana, Mr. Nano, and Mr. Maman. The Si Unyil collection at Gubug Wayang Museum was transferred directly by Drs. Suyadi and his extended family in several stages, beginning in 2015 and continuing to the present. The original designs of the characters are also displayed at Gubug Wayang Museum in the form of framed sketches depicting their various facial expressions. <\p>
